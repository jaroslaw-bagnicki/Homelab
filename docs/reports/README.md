# Reports

[← Back to Home](../README.md)

---

Periodic self-assessments of how the repository is run — process, documentation,
infrastructure code, and operational readiness. Generated by the
[`quality-assessment`](../../.github/prompts/quality-assessment.prompt.md) prompt.

**Operational reports** also live here: measured results from drills and acceptance
tests. They are evidence for the runbooks and the decision log rather than
assessments of how the repository is run.

Reports are **point-in-time snapshots**, not policy: they record what looked wrong
on the day they were written. They are **not** authoritative for design rationale —
that is the [decision log](../decisions/README.md). A report changes nothing by
itself; it becomes a plan only once its findings are turned into issues or rows in
[Overview → What's Next](../overview.md).
